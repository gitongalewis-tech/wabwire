WABWIRE — DARAJA API CLIENT CHECKLIST
====================================

Use this checklist to collect the business details and Safaricom Daraja access
needed to configure and verify Wabwire payments. Do not write live credentials
in this document.

CURRENT PAYMENT SCOPE
---------------------
The project currently supports:
- Lipa Na M-Pesa Online (STK Push) for membership payments.
- A manual Paybill receipt submission flow that an admin reviews.

Automated C2B Paybill validation/confirmation callbacks and M-Pesa B2C
disbursements to members are not currently implemented. If either is required,
confirm that as additional integration scope before onboarding.

1. BUSINESS AND PAYMENT DETAILS
-------------------------------
[ ] Registered business or organization name used for the Safaricom account.
[ ] Name and contact details of the person authorized to manage the Daraja
    account and approve production access.
[ ] Confirm which M-Pesa receiving account Wabwire will use:
    [ ] Paybill     [ ] Buy Goods/Till     [ ] Other: ____________________
[ ] The exact shortcode (Paybill or Till number) assigned to that account.
[ ] Confirm that the account belongs to, or is authorized for, the business.
[ ] Membership price to charge (KES) and any different plans or fees.
[ ] Payment account reference the payer should see/use (for example, a member
    number or Wabwire membership reference).
[ ] Confirm the intended behavior after payment: activate membership only after
    Daraja confirms a successful payment; explain any exceptions.
[ ] Refund, reversal, duplicate-payment, and payment-support procedures.
[ ] Business contact email and phone for payment-related escalations.

2. DARAJA APP ACCESS AND CREDENTIALS
-----------------------------------
Please provide access through a secure credential-sharing method, or have the
account owner create/share the app credentials securely. We need separate
credentials for each environment:

SANDBOX
[ ] Daraja sandbox app Consumer Key.
[ ] Daraja sandbox app Consumer Secret.
[ ] Sandbox shortcode and Lipa Na M-Pesa Online Passkey for that shortcode.
[ ] Confirmation that the sandbox app has the Lipa Na M-Pesa Online product
    enabled.
[ ] Sandbox test phone numbers and any test scenarios or expected results.

PRODUCTION (when ready to go live)
[ ] Production Consumer Key and Consumer Secret for the approved production
    app.
[ ] Production shortcode and matching Lipa Na M-Pesa Online Passkey.
[ ] Confirmation that Safaricom has approved the app and enabled Lipa Na M-Pesa
    Online for the production shortcode.
[ ] Confirmation of the production transaction type supported by the account:
    Paybill or Buy Goods/Till.
[ ] A phone number authorized for a small, agreed production test payment.

The shortcode and passkey must belong to the same production account. Tell us
whether the account is a Paybill or Till so we can confirm the correct Daraja
transaction type and configuration before testing.

3. CALLBACK AND NETWORK REQUIREMENTS
------------------------------------
Wabwire's STK Push callback endpoint is:

https://wabwire-backend.vercel.app/api/v1/payments/mpesa/callback

[ ] Confirm the deployed backend URL to use. If a custom backend domain is
    configured, provide it so the callback address can be updated.
[ ] Confirm whether the client's Daraja onboarding requires callback URLs to
    be registered in the portal or approved by Safaricom.
[ ] Confirm any callback security, domain, TLS, or IP allow-list requirements.
    Vercel serverless outbound IPs may not be fixed; tell us if Safaricom
    requires a static outbound IP so hosting can be planned accordingly.
[ ] Confirm whether the client needs customer-initiated C2B Paybill payments
    with separate Validation and Confirmation URLs. That flow is not part of
    the current implementation and needs additional development.

The callback must be publicly reachable over HTTPS. Do not use localhost or a
temporary development URL for production callbacks.

4. IF THE CLIENT EXPECTS PAYOUTS TO MEMBERS
------------------------------------------
The current payment feature collects membership payments; it does not send
commissions or other funds to members through Daraja B2C. If member payouts are
required, ask the client to confirm this separately and obtain:

[ ] Written confirmation that Daraja B2C disbursement is required.
[ ] Safaricom approval and the B2C product enabled for the production account.
[ ] The relevant business shortcode and authorized initiator details.
[ ] The B2C security credential/certificate required by Safaricom.
[ ] Approved result and timeout callback URLs and any network requirements.
[ ] Payout limits, approval rules, reconciliation process, and test plan.

These are sensitive credentials and require a separate secure implementation;
do not send them in this checklist or ordinary email.

5. TESTING AND GO-LIVE SIGN-OFF
-------------------------------
[ ] Test a successful sandbox STK prompt and callback.
[ ] Test a declined, cancelled, and timed-out payment.
[ ] Confirm duplicate callbacks cannot activate or charge a member twice.
[ ] Confirm the amount, shortcode, payer phone, and receipt are reconciled.
[ ] Confirm membership activates only after a verified successful payment.
[ ] Client approves the production test and go-live date.
[ ] Client identifies who will monitor payments and handle reconciliation.

SECURITY: SHARING AND STORAGE
-----------------------------
- Never put keys, secrets, passkeys, initiator credentials, or certificates in
  this file, source code, Git, screenshots, or a browser/frontend setting.
- Do not send live credentials through ordinary email or chat. Use a password
  manager or another secure, private transfer method and share access only with
  the people responsible for deployment.
- Live secrets belong only in the backend's private hosting environment. Keep
  sandbox and production credentials separate, restrict access, and rotate any
  credential that has been exposed.
